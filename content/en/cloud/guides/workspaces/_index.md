Workspaces and environments are how an organization decides which teams can read which connections and where its designs live. For the model, read [Workspaces]({{< ref "cloud/concepts/spaces/workspaces.md" >}}) and [Environments]({{< ref "cloud/concepts/spaces/environments.md" >}}). For limits, defaults, and error messages, see [Spaces]({{< ref "cloud/concepts/spaces/_index.md" >}}).

| I want to | Go to | Permission needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create, rename, or delete a workspace | [Managing Workspaces]({{< ref "cloud/guides/workspaces/managing-workspaces/index.md" >}}) | Create Workspace, Edit Workspace, Delete Workspace |
| Give a team read-only access to a workspace's connections | [Assign Teams to a Workspace]({{< ref "cloud/guides/workspaces/managing-workspaces/index.md#assign-teams-to-a-workspace" >}}) | Assign team to workspace |
| Move a design or view into a workspace | [Move Designs and Views Between Workspaces]({{< ref "cloud/guides/workspaces/managing-workspaces/index.md#move-designs-and-views-between-workspaces" >}}) | Assign Designs to Workspaces, Assign Views to Workspace |
| See who changed a workspace | [View Recent Activity]({{< ref "cloud/guides/workspaces/managing-workspaces/index.md#view-recent-activity" >}}) | View Workspace |
| Create an environment and fill it with connections | [Managing Environments]({{< ref "cloud/guides/workspaces/managing-environments/index.md" >}}) | Create Environment, Assign connections to environment |
| Make an environment's connections readable by a team | [Link Environments to a Workspace]({{< ref "cloud/guides/workspaces/managing-workspaces/index.md#link-environments-to-a-workspace" >}}) | Assign environment to workspace |
| Move a design from staging to production | [Promoting a Design from Staging to Prod]({{< ref "cloud/guides/tutorials/promoting-a-design.md" >}}) | Assign Designs to Workspaces in the destination organization |
| Share a private design with a person | [Sharing a Workspace]({{< ref "cloud/guides/tutorials/sharing-a-workspace.md" >}}) and [Sharing Designs]({{< ref "kanvas/designer/sharing/index.md" >}}) | Share Design |
