Use this section when you need an exact answer: whether a feature is in your plan, whether your role can perform an action, what a control does, which limit applies, or how to reach support. Reference pages list facts and defaults. They do not teach a workflow or argue a design.

- **Can Kanvas do it, and on which plan?** Start with [Feature Availability by Plan]({{< ref "kanvas/reference/feature-availability.md" >}}).
- **Can I do it, given my role?** See [Permissions by Role]({{< ref "kanvas/reference/permissions.md" >}}).
- **How do I open a link with a given render mode, or what does each mode draw?** See [URL Parameters]({{< ref "kanvas/reference/url-parameters.md" >}}), [Performance Limits]({{< ref "kanvas/reference/performance.md" >}}) and [Auto-pan on Drag]({{< ref "kanvas/reference/autopan.md" >}}).
- **How do I reach someone?** See [Help and Support]({{< ref "kanvas/reference/help-and-support.md" >}}).

For learning and task-oriented content, see [Getting Started]({{< ref "kanvas/getting-started/_index.md" >}}), the [How-to Guides]({{< ref "kanvas/guides/_index.md" >}}) and the [Concepts]({{< ref "kanvas/concepts/_index.md" >}}).
